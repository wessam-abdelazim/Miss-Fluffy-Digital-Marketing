
# Miss Fluffy
## Digital Marketing Strategy & Social Media Growth Project

---

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** Miss Fluffy - Digital Marketing Project

**Brand:** Miss Fluffy Hair Care Products

**Project Type:** Digital Marketing Graduation Project

**Track:** Digital Marketing Specialist - DEPI

### Project Description
This project aims to develop a digital marketing strategy for Miss Fluffy Hair Care Products to strengthen brand awareness, improve social media presence, increase audience engagement, and attract relevant audiences interested in hair care.

The project includes market research, audience analysis, competitor analysis, marketing strategy, content planning, creative design, campaign planning, and performance measurement.

---

## 2. Project Idea

Developing an integrated digital marketing plan for Miss Fluffy Hair Care Products, focusing on understanding the target audience, analyzing the market and competitors, creating engaging social media content, planning digital campaigns, and measuring marketing performance through relevant KPIs.

## 6. KPIs

The project will monitor relevant digital marketing performance indicators, including:

- Reach
- Impressions
- Engagement Rate
- Follower Growth
- Profile Visits
- Reels Views
- Website Traffic
- Messages and Inquiries
- Content Performance

KPIs will be evaluated based on available data and campaign objectives.
